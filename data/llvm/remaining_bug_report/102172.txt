Comment by aykevl: I can confirm this issue: https://godbolt.org/z/vEhhs774T
It is indeed a bug because it breaks ABI compatibility with avr-gcc (and it unnecessarily increases alignment requirements while no such requirements exist on AVR).

Interestingly `short` has an alignment of 2, while `int` and `long` (correctly) have an alignment of 1.
Also, `long long` has an alignment of 1 in Clang (correct), but 8 in the LLVM IR (incorrect). That would definitely lead to miscompilations when calculating struct offsets for example.
