Comment by efriedma-quic: Crashing at https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/blob/main/clang/lib/CodeGen/CodeGenModule.cpp#L5713 .  I think the issue is that we're modifying the use-list while we're iterating over the use-list.  Probably instead of calling eraseFromParent() in the loop that iterates over the use-list, we need to save the old calls in an vector, and delete them in a separate loop.
