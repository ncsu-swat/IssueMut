Issue Title: [clang] move -Wcast-function-type under -Wextra
Original Post: via https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/issues/74617, I was looking into why I observe this diagnostic with gcc but not clang. It looks like our project (llvmlibc) builds with -Wextra but not -Wcast-function-type.   GCC 8 added support for that flag, and did so under -Wextra.
