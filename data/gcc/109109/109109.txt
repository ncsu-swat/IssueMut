    other f* functions in those tests), and RA ends up with:
    (insn 11 10 17 2 (set (reg:DI 0 ax [89])
            (ior:DI (ashift:DI (zero_extend:DI (mem:SI (plus:SI (mult:SI (reg:SI 0 ax [94])
