After updating to gcc 14.1 we noticed that many jump tables were no longer being optimised to use Thumb2 table branch instructions (tbb/tbh) compared to 13.2.

A bisect shows the problem seems to have been introduced by 7006e5d2d7 "arm: Use deltas for Arm switch tables".
