- User: Richard Biener | Time: 2024-04-24 13:04:37 UTC
  Text: The issue is that unloop_loops doesn't work when to unloop loops are nested.  The unroller takes great care of working on a single loop level at a time, cleaning up the CFG and fixing loops inbetween but we fail to ensure this here.

unlooping in CH is somewhat a wrong tool for the wrong task as it doesn't adhere to constraints present.  There's the possibility this can be salvaged by making unloop_loops more robust, in particular computing loop bodies (Which is fragile) up-front.

But it would be much nicer if unlooping would simply add __builtin_unreachable () as the first stmt into the latch and leave the rest to CFG cleanup.
