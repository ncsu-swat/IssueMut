- User: Haochen Jiang | Time: 2023-08-18 02:54:20 UTC
  Text: It is caused by when including immintrin.h, since the pragma is removed, there will be no AVX support, which makes _mm256_setzero_pd invisible.

Adding a AVX2 pragma instead of removing it should solve the problem.

I am working a patch on that.
