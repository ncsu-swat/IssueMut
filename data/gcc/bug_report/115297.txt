  Text: Createdattachment 58315[details]Proposed patch

any_divmod instructions are modelled with invalid RTX:

  [(set (match_operand:DI 0 "register_operand" "=c")
        (sign_extend:DI (match_operator:SI 3 "divmod_operator"
                        [(match_operand:DI 1 "register_operand" "a")
                         (match_operand:DI 2 "register_operand" "b")])))
   (clobber (reg:DI 23))
   (clobber (reg:DI 28))]

SImode divmod_operator (div,mod,udiv,umod) has DImode operands.

The patch adds truncate:SI to the input operand to make machine modes consistent.
