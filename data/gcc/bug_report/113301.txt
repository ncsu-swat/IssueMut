- User: Andrew Pinski | Time: 2024-01-10 22:25:17 UTC
  Text: I have a patch which disables the "simplification" of "1/x" for signed until late which solves this missed optimization. I will post it once it finishes testing.

Note we already delayed the simplification of `bool * d` to `bool?A:0` until late for similar reasons, seePR 103257on that.
