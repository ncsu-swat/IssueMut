- User: Richard Biener | Time: 2023-03-29 07:51:15 UTC
  Text: It's really an old issue I think.  We record a lattice in forwprop, marking
stmts to be removed.  But the early forwprop phase runs on the IL with
the stmt operands not substituted, instead relying on old get_prop_source_stmt and remove_prop_source_from_use.  That mechanism can interfere with the
lattice recorded stmt removal.

Testing a patch.
