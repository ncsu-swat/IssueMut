- User: Richard Biener | Time: 2023-06-19 07:53:53 UTC
  Text: I will have a look.  OK, so we unroll ("unloop") the middle-loop and the
recorded loop father now no longer covers the sub-loops.  We then end up
value-numbering a part of the CFG that has defs used for later loop nb_iterations but value-numbering only substitutes into loop info of
loops in the region it processes.

Now, we free all nb_iterations but only after the CFG cleanup.  The simple
fix is to do that beforehand.
